Amuratsu brings manga, novels, fan fiction and anime into one app.

A Material You home screen, search across media types, reading history and local imports help you pick up where you left off. Light, dark, AMOLED and reading themes are available.

Amuratsu is an early-stage open-source fork of Kototoro, originally derived from Kotatsu. Extension support and readers are inherited from those projects. No media content is bundled.
